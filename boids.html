HISTORY OF BOIDS ALGORITHM

Boids algorithm, developed by Craig Reynold in 1986, is an artificial life simulation that mimics the flocking patterns of birds. Though simple, the algorithm is a decentralized, self-organized system of autonomous agents. By tweaking the simple rules for the algorithm, the autonomous agents can emulate different animals and behaviours. "Boid" is derived from bird-oid meaning bird-like, where -oid suffix is latin for like, similar to how humanoid mean human-like.

SEEKING

how boids steer

the boid applies a steering force equal to its desired velocity minus its current velocity.

fleeting behaviour

for fleeting behaviour, the desired steering velocity is the same just pointing in the oposite direction. this is done by multiplying the velocity by -1
